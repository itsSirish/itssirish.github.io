About Me

Hiya, I'm Sirish Visweswar 👋, a machine-learning researcher and engineer with an M.S. in Computer Science from NYU. I'm a Research Engineer at the Neuroinformatics Lab with Prof. Erdem Varol, where I build self-supervised models for large-scale neural and imaging data.

My recent work has two threads. The first is localizing electrodes in the brain directly from the signals they record: geometry-aware self-supervised foundation models on raw Neuropixels LFP, published this year as two NeurIPS 2026 papers (a main-track paper and the LFP2Gene workshop paper). The second is high-speed voltage imaging on a 440 Hz two-photon microscope: I built its image reconstruction for the OJAMI paper (under review at Nature Methods), and a self-supervised denoiser that recovers individual action potentials from shot noise without ever seeing clean ground truth.

My interests sit at the intersection of deep learning, computer vision, and neuroscience, but I've also dabbled in reinforcement learning, game development, full-stack apps, blockchain, and NLP.

Outside of tech, I'm into creating art, which keeps me grounded and feeds into my problem-solving style: a mix of creativity, structure, and curiosity.
