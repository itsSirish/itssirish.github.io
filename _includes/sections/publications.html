Geometry-Aware Self-Supervised Electrophysiology Representation Learning | NeurIPS 2026 (Main Track) · Co-first author

2026 View Paper

Localizing recording electrodes in 3D directly from raw single-channel LFP, without histology, using probe geometry as a self-supervisory signal.

  • Geometry-aware contrastive objectives (CCFDistc, ChanDistc) constrain the embedding space to mirror physical channel layout along the probe
  • Benchmarks three audio SSL backbones (Wav2Vec 2.0, Whisper, Data2Vec) against supervised AnyNet, ViT, and spectral baselines
  • State of the art in brain-region classification and 3D coordinate regression across datasets, labs, species, and probe technologies; gains compound at larger data scales

LFP2Gene: Recovering Local Molecular Composition from Local Field Potentials | NeurIPS 2026 BrainBodyFM Workshop · Co-first author

2026 View Paper

Self-supervised LFP representations retain signatures of the local molecular environment around the electrode.

  • Bridges IBL Neuropixels channels to Allen MERFISH atlases through the Common Coordinate Framework; a frozen encoder predicts expression of 1,122 genes
  • Recovers a good deal of local gene expression on 34 held-out subjects, more than any alternative under the same frozen-readout protocol
  • Shared structure is low-dimensional (PLS and CCA agree on five dimensions) and organised at the scale of brain regions

OJAMI: Electro-Optic Jitter Accelerated Multiphoton Imaging | Nature Methods (under review) · Co-author

2026

An add-on electro-optic scan module giving standard two-photon microscopes an order-of-magnitude speed-up at subcellular resolution.

  • Resonates along the mechanical scanner's slow axis, fast enough to record membrane voltage at 440 Hz
  • My contribution: image reconstruction, mapping each laser pulse's scattered sample onto a regular grid with an adaptive-bandwidth Gaussian kernel
  • S. Tucker, T. Reizis, E. Guralnik, D. Theagene, K. S. Guillory, V. S. Parupudi, D. Rinberg, F. St-Pierre, E. Varol & S. Shoham

A Smartphone-Enabled Deep Learning Approach for Myocardial Infarction Detection Using ECG Traces for IoT-Based Healthcare Applications | IEEE Sensors Letters, vol. 7, no. 11 · First author

November 2023 View Paper

Ensemble deep learning model for real-time myocardial infarction detection on mobile devices using ECG traces.

  • Developed majority voting ensemble with five quantized transfer learning architectures (FP16 and INT8 formats)
  • Achieved 99.34% accuracy using fivefold cross-validation scheme for mobile health imaging
  • Created portable smartphone-based healthcare system for real-time MI and heart abnormality detection
